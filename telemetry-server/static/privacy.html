CarRadio Privacy

Last updated: 2025.

What we collect

  • A random device identifier (generated on your device, not linked to you personally)
  • Firmware version and reset reason on each boot
  • Connection events (WiFi connect/disconnect, Bluetooth connect/disconnect)
  • Stream open/close events with HTTP status code
  • Audio underrun counts
  • OTA update phases
  • Hashed radio station titles (we cannot reverse the hash)
  • Hashed Bluetooth MAC addresses (hashed with a per-device secret — we cannot cross-link devices)

What we do NOT collect

  • Your WiFi password
  • Your location
  • Audio content
  • Raw Bluetooth MAC addresses
  • Any personal data

How long we keep it

  • Event records: 90 days, then automatically deleted
  • Uploaded diagnostic logs: 30 days, then automatically deleted

Opting out

Open the CarRadio setup portal on your device and uncheck "Help improve CarRadio by sending anonymous diagnostic data". Local logging continues so you can still download your own log.

Deleting your data

Use the Delete my data button in the CarRadio portal. This immediately removes all stored events, sessions, and logs associated with your device from our servers.
